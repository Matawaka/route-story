Original 110515741B ZIP/GeoTIFF remain ignored. `scripts/terrain/prepare.py` crops/reprojects with rasterio1.4.4/pyproj3.7.2, bilinearly samples real elevations, rounds to signed integer metres, and reserves -32768 for missing data. No missing height becomes sea level. Production contains two bounded grids only:50m Standard and100m Compatibility over a30×40km local scene, origin6.2°E/61.28°N. Original source is10m, so the derived grid is deliberately coarser; no satellite texture or inferred roads/settlements.

The local east/north tangent approximation uses6371008.8m radius, wrapped longitude and origin cosine. It is consistent between preprocessing, GPS placement and camera. Within this bounded region the approximation is intended for visualisation, not surveying. Distance/ascent still use the unchanged GPX/Haversine implementation. Source GeoTIFF has EPSG25833 horizontal CRS, but no explicit vertical CRS;2020 metadata likewise specifies only2D CRS. Vertical datum is therefore **unspecified**, no NN2000/ellipsoid conversion is assumed. GPX elevation/time fields are never overwritten by DEM samples.

`TerrainDataset` validates finite grid bounds, resolution, dimensions, units,1:1 vertical scale, sample range[-500,9000]m and at most500000 vertices. Loader accepts only the reviewed local filenames, ≤1000000B/grid, and verifies SHA256 before use. Bilinear sampling rejects missing corners. Triangular sampling matches the exact mesh diagonals for route draping/LOS. Missing cells are omitted, not filled with fake geometry. Route coverage checks include edges and a2km horizontal guard band, with bounded sample work. Excessive drape geometry fails explicitly and leaves the 2D choice available.

## Camera and output

`createTerrainCameraPlan` prepares241 distance-indexed shots plus exact segment-boundary pairs (≤128 segments), using route direction/look-ahead and actual nearby relief. Horizontal symmetric smoothing and local upper altitude envelope are confined to a GPX segment. The pure `getTerrainCameraStateAt` uses StoryTimeline phase/progress, never the previous frame. Intro descends from a perspective-fitted caption-safe overview; replay follows the genuine point with controlled oblique bearing; outro returns to the full overview. A cut/fade at segment boundaries does not interpolate a fictitious route. Auto examines neighbourhood relief along the route (≥100m), not mountains elsewhere in the pack; explicit3D can present a covered flat/stationary route calmly.

The altitude envelope is conservatively ≥ the maximum DEM height+350m for every keyframe and every interpolated camera position. This guarantees camera clearance even between sampled timestamps within the bounded mesh, at the cost of less dramatic low valley flights. LOS checks every vertical/horizontal grid crossing and every triangle diagonal, including the clearance-ramp breakpoint. Height is linear within each triangular patch, so interval endpoints bound the entire ray against this exact quantised mesh; this does not prove safety against finer real terrain absent from the derived DEM. Final state rechecks the ray, independent of seeking history. No-data rays fail instead of inventing terrain. Independent dense-ray tests exercise cliffs, valleys, passes and ridges.

The depth-tested ribbon subdivides every original GPX edge and samples terrain independently of GPS elevation. No GPX vertices/segments are removed; the model is a ground drape, not an inferred bridge/tunnel altitude. Scene contains ≤500000 terrain vertices and≤400000 ribbon vertices. One WebGL destination-resolution canvas is captured to the existing2D output canvas, with sequential Mediabunny backpressure and unchanged360p/720p,24fps,10/20/30s,720-frame,32MiB limits. No pre-captured frame archive. Different GPU/browser implementations are not promised byte-identical; direct/backward seeking and preview/export equivalence are tested within a controlled renderer.

Shader preparation is checked before enabling export. Shader failure, incomplete framebuffer or lost context rejects the frame; no partial scene is declared successful. Every mesh/material/context is disposed; quality/route changes recreate the bounded scene. Context loss offers explicit2D selection or recreation by selecting3D again. No silent downgrade of selected3D or encoder quality. Auto chooses3D only for complete covered terrain with relief, otherwise explains its2D selection. Canvas Cinematic and Classic remain intact.

## Visual limitations to review

The ribbon has an additional depth-tested1px centre trace,≤200000 vertices, independent pairs per original edge and the same distance/progress shader. It improves distant-route continuity without drawing through mountains. Real-height100m contour shading and regional-edge fade are presentation, not weather or surveyed water classification. Intro/outro gaze is clamped above exact mesh+24m before LOS; below-ground inputs and non-finite/>80km flight reject. Actual full-timeline audit caught a near-target singularity missed by replay-only tests, now fixed; all decoded geographic frames are checked in addition to shader/readiness. Unique frame hashes alone could miss a blank map behind a changing HUD.

Local pack edges can remain visible in wide overview shots; this is a regional terrain model, not worldwide coverage. Sea-level colour is a height treatment, not a water classification/bathymetric claim. Terrain lighting is deterministic art direction, not historical sun/weather. Conservative flight height, derived-grid rather than source10m collision bounds, long-title ellipsis, steep-slope ribbon continuity and the final composition need actual decoded-frame review. Physical mobile3D/GPU support is not inferred from desktop tests.
